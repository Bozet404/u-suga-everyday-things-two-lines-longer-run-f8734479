## What it does

`main.py` writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

1. Line 1 explains rain to a 10-year-old in a single vivid sentence built
   on one analogy: the sky wringing out a giant sponge it soaked up from
   the sea.
2. Line 2 says in one sentence why that analogy works — what it maps
   (clouds gathering water and releasing it slowly) and what it leaves out
   (clouds are floating water vapour, not cloth, and wind and cold, not
   hands, squeeze the rain out).

The output is plain UTF-8 text, two lines, well within the 1–400
character gate.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
